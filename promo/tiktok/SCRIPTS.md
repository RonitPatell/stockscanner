**Say at the end of every VO (keep on screen ~3s+):**  
“For informational purposes only. Not financial advice.”

## Ads / Spark checklist (scripts + creative)
- Age gate **18+** if running paid.
- Landing page should match the ad: **product / demo /today /pricing** — not “get rich.”
- Prefer CTAs: “Try the free briefing,” “See the demo,” “Start free trial.”
- Do **not** say you’ll make money, beat the market, or recover losses.
- Paid finance ads may still need TikTok **Industry Entry / financial services approval** depending on market — this script style reduces creative rejection risk; it doesn’t replace licensing requirements where they apply.

## Safe hashtags (optional, 3–5)
`#productivity` `#markets` `#financetok` `#software` `#workflow`  
Skip: `#daytrading` `#makemoney` `#signals` `#passiveincome` when running paid.
